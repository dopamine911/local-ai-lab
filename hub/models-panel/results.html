How this page works

Every result comes from MLflow, where each test run is logged. Pick a test at the top. The first box shows the latest result for each model; the bars compare them. Click a model to see its history and every run's note.

Tick two results to put them side by side, from the same model (before and after a change) or from two models. Green means better, red means worse; for some numbers lower is better (time, misses), and the page knows which.

Tool use: three seeds of the same settings form one result (their average, with the lowest and highest). Only the full suite counts here; quick 30-point runs are left out.

"In use now" means the model is in a llama-swap config today. All the numbers are also in tuning/results.md and in MLflow itself.
